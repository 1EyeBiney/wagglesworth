# Copy review

Every line of interface copy written in a character's voice lives here first, per
CLAUDE.md. None of it is canon until Barb approves, edits, or replaces it. Each entry
says where it's used, what it's drafted from, and gives at least one alternative.

To change one: edit the live text directly wherever it's used on the site (the file
path is given below each entry), then update this file to say what she chose and mark
the entry **Approved**.

## Front gates: Baroness Wagglesworth's welcome

**Used in:** `site/index.njk`
**Status:** Draft, not yet reviewed by Barb.
**Drafted from:** the established voice in story 07 (`07_baroness_origin_story.md`) —
her formal title ("Her Grace, Baroness Wagglesworth"), the running joke about her
chair/throne, and the short, dry, self-important tone every source document asks for.
Nothing here states a fact about her not already in the stories or the character bible.

**Chosen for the live page:**
> "Her Grace, Baroness Wagglesworth, welcomes you — finally — to Wagglesworth Estate.
> Wipe your paws. Mind the roses. And whatever you do, do not sit in my chair."

**Alternatives, if Barb prefers a different flavor:**

> "Oh, you're here. Wonderful. I'm Baroness Wagglesworth, and this is my Estate —
> drawing room, gardens, one very suspicious hidden ballroom, and a household that
> mostly does what I say. Enter, if you dare to be charmed."

> "Welcome to Wagglesworth Estate. I am Baroness Wagglesworth — Supreme Ruler of
> Fabulousness, snark queen of the drawing room, and, apparently, your host for the
> evening. Do come in."
