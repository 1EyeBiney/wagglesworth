CORE PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS

- The public name is Wagglesworth Estate. Never use “Noodleverse” in public-facing copy, metadata, routes, or interface labels.
- Credit the project as “Created by Barb.” The About page should say only that the stories began in an online Facebook community.
- The visual direction is elegant royal estate plus whimsical illustrated storybook.
- Use royal purple, soft pink, silver, and cream. Absolutely no gold, gold gradients, champagne gold, or yellow-metal ornament.
- Use the Silver Rose as the permanent crest and a subtle repeating motif.
- Stories, characters, and artwork are equally important.
- Accessibility is a foundation. Target WCAG 2.2 AA and implement semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader support, responsive reflow, useful alt text, reduced-motion behavior, sufficient contrast, and a working A− / A+ / Reset text-size control.
- Do not autoplay music or audio.
- Do not add comments, a guestbook, accounts, a Facebook button, an admin system, or audio narration in the initial release.
- Do not invent character biography, relationships, appearance traits, titles, sayings, or story events. Unknown information must remain marked for confirmation.

SITE STRUCTURE

Build these primary destinations:

1. Home / Front Gates
2. Stories
3. Meet the Residents
4. Explore the Estate
5. Estate Gallery
6. Creating Your Own Noodles Adventure with AI
7. About
8. Accessibility, Privacy, and Site Map utility pages

The opening experience should show the front gates/exterior with Baroness Wagglesworth welcoming visitors in a short, punchy, funny voice. The primary action is “Enter Wagglesworth Estate.” Do not show a newest-story preview before entry. After entry, provide clear paths to the main areas and a data-driven Newest Adventure feature.

Use an illustrated overhead Estate map with semantic, keyboard-accessible named hotspots. Every location must also be reachable through accessible page structure. On small screens, use a simplified map plus named destination cards; do not require precision tapping, dragging, or pinching.

Organize stories by series and category rather than by date. Keep multi-part stories together in episode order. Use title-led archive views, one readable column on story pages, scene artwork near the matching passage, an optional end gallery, and deterministic Previous/Next controls. Search must search stories only.

Group the resident directory into Main Household, Ladies-in-Waiting, Friends & Family, and Special/Guest Characters. Baroness appears first and receives the featured profile. Do not automatically show every story appearance on character pages in the first release.

The Estate Gallery must support Characters, Everyday Adventures, Tales from Wagglesworth Estate, Special Events, and Behind-the-Scenes/Reference Images. Every published image needs a descriptive filename, dimensions, provenance/source type, public-use approval, caption where useful, and contextual alt text.

Seasonal entrance scenes may change the background, Baroness’s outfit, welcome line, and one gentle animation. They must preserve the same navigation, labels, focus order, contrast, and layout. Honor reduced-motion preferences and provide a static fallback.
