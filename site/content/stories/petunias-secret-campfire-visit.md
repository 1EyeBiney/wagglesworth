Mom had said Petunia was too little for the campfire.

Her Grace, Baroness Wagglesworth, considered this an opinion rather than a final ruling.

Before the family left, Noodles placed her travel bag beside the tiny doll.

Petunia climbed inside.

Buttons the Bear saw the entire operation.

“This is unauthorized,” he whispered.

The Baroness stared at him.

Buttons sighed and zipped the bag halfway closed.

“Security escort,” he corrected.

At the forest lake, the campfire began to glow. The family settled into their chairs. The Baroness was placed safely away from the flames, and Buttons sat nearby.

Then the travel bag moved.

One tiny hand appeared.

Petunia climbed out wearing a paper camping hat.

Buttons gasped much too loudly.

Mom turned toward the sound.

The Baroness quickly positioned herself in front of Petunia.

“Why is your bag moving?” Mom asked.

“Woodland breeze.”

There was no breeze.

Petunia sneezed.

Mom found her immediately.

For one long moment, nobody spoke.

Then Mom laughed.

Petunia was allowed to stay as long as she remained safely in the bag, beside Noodles, and far from the fire. Buttons was assigned to guard them both.

The three watched the flames together while the family laughed around the lake.

The Baroness never admitted she had planned the smuggling operation.

Buttons never admitted he had helped.

Petunia wore the paper camping hat all the way home.
