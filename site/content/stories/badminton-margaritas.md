Her Grace, Baroness Wagglesworth, believed every royal summer required an afternoon of healthy outdoor activity.

She also believed she should supervise that activity from a shaded chair while holding a cold drink.

Thus began the Wagglesworth Estate Badminton Soirée.

The garden court was decorated with pink ribbons and silver roses. Countess Tilly drew the boundary lines twice because Buttons walked through the first set while carrying the net backward.

“Security inspection,” Buttons explained.

“You are wearing the net,” said Tilly.

Lady Virginia arrived with a silver pitcher of pawgaritas.

The Baroness inspected it carefully.

“For hydration,” Virginia said.

“Naturally,” said the Baroness, accepting the first glass.

The ten Ladies-in-Waiting divided into teams.

Lady Piper and Lady Siren played together, although they spent so much time giggling that the shuttlecock landed between them four times. Lady Moira and Lady Lexie formed a quiet, dangerously competent pair. Dame Muriel reviewed the official rules before every serve. Lady Isla sang encouragement from the back line. Lady Noodles Hobson carried Petunia in a tiny front basket, and Petunia waved a miniature racket that was not much larger than a spoon.

Lady Special Sparkle wore a glittering sports gown.

“That cannot possibly be practical,” Tilly said.

Special Sparkle served. A cloud of glitter followed the shuttlecock over the net.

“But it is visible,” she replied.

Lady Astrid played on roller skates.

“Is that allowed?” Piper asked.

Dame Muriel searched the rulebook.

“It does not specifically say no.”

Astrid rolled from one side of the court to the other, returning every shot until one skate caught a ribbon. She spun three times, recovered, and sent the shuttlecock directly into Buttons’s security hat.

Buttons stood at attention with the feathered end sticking from the top.

Esme laughed.

Buttons decided to leave it there.

The final match placed the Baroness and Tilly against Moira and Lexie.

“I thought you were supervising,” Tilly said.

“I am leading by example.”

The Baroness set down her pawgarita, lifted the racket, and served with great royal force.

The shuttlecock traveled backward.

It landed in Virginia’s pitcher.

Everyone stared.

Virginia calmly poured another glass around it.

“Point to the pawgaritas,” she said.

The Baroness declared the scoring system flawed and changed the event from a tournament to an exhibition.

By late afternoon, nobody knew which team had won. Piper and Siren were laughing on the grass. Moira and Lexie were still debating the final point. Dame Muriel had added several new rules. Isla was singing. Special Sparkle had decorated the net. Astrid was skating circles around the court. Lady Noodles Hobson had tucked a sleepy Petunia beneath a tiny blanket.

Buttons brought Esme a flower from the hedge and claimed it had been recovered during a security operation.

The Baroness leaned back in her chair.

“A successful day of athletics,” she announced.

Tilly looked at the untouched racket beside her.

“You played for forty seconds.”

“And remained undefeated.”

Lady Virginia raised the pitcher.

Everyone toasted to friendship, sunshine, and the remarkable ability of Wagglesworth Estate to turn any sport into a garden party.
