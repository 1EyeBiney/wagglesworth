Snow covered the windows, the driveway, and every outdoor plan the household had made.

Mom was working, so the three remaining household strategists—Baroness Wagglesworth, Buttons the Bear, and Tiny Petunia—declared it an official UNO day.

The Baroness sat at the table with her cards arranged in a perfect fan.

Buttons studied everyone with narrowed eyes.

Petunia sat on two books so she could see over the edge of the table.

The first game lasted four minutes.

The Baroness played a red eight.

Petunia played a red two.

Buttons slapped down a yellow card and looked pleased with himself.

“Wrong color,” said the Baroness.

Buttons quickly picked it back up.

“Security test.”

The second game began.

Petunia played a Skip card.

Buttons stared at her.

“Suspicious.”

Petunia blinked.

The Baroness played a Reverse.

Buttons stared at her too.

“Very suspicious.”

“It is a card game,” the Baroness said. “The cards are supposed to do things.”

Buttons held his cards close to his chest.

“Perhaps. Or perhaps everyone is cheating.”

The Baroness looked offended.

Petunia shook her tiny head.

“That is exactly what cheaters would say,” Buttons replied.

By the third game, Buttons had created security procedures.

No one could draw a card without announcing it. No one could reach beneath the table. Petunia’s sleeves were inspected, even though her dress barely had sleeves. The Baroness was required to keep both paws visible.

“I am Her Grace,” she reminded him. “I do not hide cards.”

“Royalty has motive,” Buttons said.

“What motive?”

“Winning.”

The Baroness had to admit he had a point.

The game continued.

Buttons finally placed his next-to-last card on the pile.

“UNO!” he shouted.

Petunia quietly played a Draw Four.

Buttons stared at the card.

Then at Petunia.

Then at the four cards he was required to draw.

“Tiny doll has been planning this all day.”

Petunia smiled.

The Baroness began laughing so hard she dropped half her cards onto the floor.

Buttons pointed.

“Evidence!”

“Evidence that I have paws,” the Baroness said, gathering them.

At that moment, Mom entered during a break from work and found cards everywhere, Petunia celebrating, the Baroness laughing, and one very suspicious bear demanding a full investigation.

“Who won?” Mom asked.

Petunia raised both tiny hands.

Buttons folded his arms.

“Results are under review.”

Outside, the snow kept falling.

Inside, they began another game—after Buttons counted the deck twice.
