Mom and Dad knew a secret meeting was taking place in the living room.

They knew because Baroness Wagglesworth, Buttons, and Tiny Petunia had all entered wearing white sheets with eyeholes.

The three “ghosts” sat in a circle and spoke in whispers.

Mom and Dad listened from the hallway.

Because Dad is blind, he was particularly good at following quiet voices and suspicious movement. Mom tried to lean close to the door without making it creak.

Inside, Buttons opened the meeting.

“Operation Pawgarita Patrol begins now.”

The Baroness adjusted her ghost sheet.

“Objectives: obtain candy, secure costumes, and establish unlimited pawgaritas.”

Petunia held up a hand-drawn map of the kitchen. A red line led directly to the blender.

“Tiny doll has planned blender mission,” Buttons whispered.

“Naturally,” said the Baroness. “She is the only one who can fit behind the appliance.”

In the hallway, Mom covered her mouth to keep from laughing.

Dad whispered, “Did they say unlimited pawgaritas?”

“Yes.”

“I admire their ambition.”

The meeting continued.

Buttons would guard the candy bowl. Petunia would approach the blender. The Baroness would supervise everything from a safe distance while wearing the most elegant ghost costume.

Mom tried to take a note on her phone.

VoiceOver suddenly announced at full volume:

“NOTES. NEW NOTE. OPERATION PAWGARITA PATROL.”

The living room became silent.

Dad closed his eyes.

Mom froze.

The three ghosts slowly turned toward the doorway.

“Spies,” Buttons said.

The Baroness pulled off her sheet.

“I expected better surveillance from my own parents.”

Mom and Dad entered the room. Petunia hid the kitchen map beneath her dress. Buttons sat on the candy plan.

Dad folded his arms.

“The blender mission is denied.”

“On what grounds?” asked the Baroness.

“You cannot reach the controls.”

The Baroness looked at Petunia.

Petunia looked at Buttons.

Buttons looked toward the kitchen.

Mom confiscated the map.

Operation Pawgarita Patrol ended before it began. The candy remained secure, the blender remained unplugged, and the three ghosts were permitted one supervised bowl of popcorn.

The Baroness called it a temporary tactical retreat.

Dad called it the loudest secret meeting he had ever heard.
