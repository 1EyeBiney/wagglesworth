The Great Peanut Butter Incident began with one open jar and three dogs who had not been invited to help.

Mom was preparing treats in the kitchen. Echo waited near the counter. Straxx watched from the doorway. Her Grace, Baroness Wagglesworth, sat in the middle of the floor pretending the entire event had nothing to do with her.

Then Mom stepped away for one minute.

When she returned, the spoon was gone.

Echo had peanut butter on one ear.

Straxx had it across his nose.

The Baroness had a perfect golden smear from chin to bow.

“Nobody move,” Mom said.

All three moved.

Echo shook his head. Peanut butter struck the cabinet.

Straxx backed into the trash can.

The Baroness attempted to leave the kitchen with the missing spoon.

Mom caught her at the doorway.

“Noodles!”

The Baroness released the spoon and looked deeply betrayed by the use of her common name.

The cleanup required warm water, towels, and a large amount of pet-safe foam. By the time Mom finished, Echo looked damp, Straxx looked offended, and the Baroness looked like she had survived a weather event.

Dad entered and heard three wet dogs, one tired Mom, and a towel hitting the floor.

“What happened?” he asked.

“They’re covered in foam,” Mom said.

Dad paused.

“Well, I can’t see it, but they certainly sound guilty.”

The Baroness sneezed.

Echo shook again.

Straxx tried to lick peanut butter from the cabinet.

Dad listened for another second.

“Correction. They sound extremely guilty.”

Mom lined everyone up for inspection. Echo’s ear was clean. Straxx’s nose was clean. The Baroness’s bow had survived, although it now leaned sharply to one side.

Then Dad reached into his pocket and found something sticky.

The missing peanut-butter spoon had somehow been placed in the pocket of his sweater.

Everyone became silent.

Dad lifted the spoon.

“I assume this was also the dogs?”

The Baroness looked at Echo.

Echo looked at Straxx.

Straxx looked at the floor.

Mom began laughing.

The Baroness lifted her chin and walked away with all the dignity available to someone still smelling strongly of peanut butter.

No one ever proved who placed the spoon in Dad’s pocket.

The official household report blamed “uncontrolled kitchen circumstances.”

Dad called it three guilty dogs and one very sticky mystery.
