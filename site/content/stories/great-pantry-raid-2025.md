In the pitch-black kitchen, Baroness Wagglesworth, Buttons, and Petunia launched a highly classified pantry operation. The targets were ambitious: cheddar crackers, fudge brownies, and marshmallows.

They thought darkness was on their side.

It was not.

From two rooms away, Dad heard the faintest crinkle of a chip bag. Not only did he know somebody was in the pantry, he knew exactly which shelf they were investigating and ordered the suspicious royal party to put everything back.

Noodles was appalled. How could one man hear a snack bag from two rooms away? Her Grace concluded that Dad's hearing was apparently sharper than a greater wax moth equipped with biological sonar.

Buttons immediately proposed a new strategy: socks on their heads as disguises.

Petunia, showing considerably more tactical sense, suggested they simply wait until Dad was snoring.

The Great Pantry Raid of 2025 was officially a failure.

The household had learned an important lesson: sneaking snacks past Dad was nearly impossible.

Apparently, they lived with a human bat.
