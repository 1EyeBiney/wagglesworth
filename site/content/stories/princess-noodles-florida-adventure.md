After her whirlwind adventures in the bustling cities of the north, Princess Noodles was ready for sunshine, sparkle, and a little Florida magic.

## The Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique

Her first stop was Orlando’s Bibbidi Bobbidi Boutique, where Noodles selected the complete Princess Noodles package.

Royal attendants helped her choose a magnificent gown. Her hair was styled with shimmering touches, and sparkling makeup with a tiny face gem completed the transformation. A crystal tiara sat perfectly above her ears.

Her package included a princess sash, a cinch bag for royal necessities, nail polish, and a garment bag with a satin hanger to keep the gown safe.

Noodles studied herself in the mirror.

“I look pawsitively regal.”

She turned so the skirt floated around her.

“Bow before royalty.”

The attendants did not bow, but they did tell her she looked beautiful, which Noodles considered an acceptable substitute.

## Meeting Lady

Before Grinchmas, Noodles met Lady.

Lady was even more graceful in person than Noodles had imagined. She carried herself with elegance, warmth, and the calm confidence of someone who had never once stepped in wet mulch.

“She’s even more graceful in person!” Noodles whispered.

Lady gave her a special **Stay Pawsitive** charm as a reminder to be brave, kind, and hopeful on every adventure.

Noodles held the charm close.

“This is the best day of my life!”

She placed it safely with her other royal treasures.

## Grinchmas at Universal

Next came Universal’s Grinchmas celebration.

Whoville was filled with colorful decorations, music, lights, and wonderfully unusual holiday cheer. Princess Noodles attended the Grinchmas Who-liday Spectacular in her finest style.

“This sounds like a lovely show,” she said as it began.

Then the Grinch appeared.

Noodles watched every dramatic expression, every complaint about Christmas, and every clever bit of mischief.

She leaned toward Mom.

“Maybe I should take some notes… just in case I need to steal Christmas someday.”

Mom reminded her that royalty was generally expected to give gifts rather than steal them.

Noodles decided she preferred receiving them anyway.

## A Beach Escape at New Smyrna

After the noise and excitement of Orlando, Noodles took an Uber to New Smyrna Beach.

She enjoyed fresh seafood at Breakers while the ocean rolled nearby. The meal was delicious, the evening air was warm, and Noodles considered ordering a second dinner in the interest of coastal research.

That night, she walked along the beach beneath the moon. The waves shone silver. Cool sand pressed beneath her paws. Lady’s **Stay Pawsitive** charm sparkled beside her princess sash.

The trip ended in a cozy beach bungalow, where Noodles curled up after the fullest royal day imaginable.

She had become a princess, met Lady, celebrated Grinchmas, eaten beside the ocean, and walked a moonlit beach.

As she drifted to sleep, her crystal tiara rested safely nearby and her gown hung from its satin hanger.

Florida had given Princess Noodles magic, friendship, holiday mischief, and the sea.

She was already planning her return.
