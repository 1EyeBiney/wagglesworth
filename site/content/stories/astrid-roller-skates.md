Lady Astrid had been accidentally left out of one royal adventure.

She did not complain.

She made a palace correction.

Wearing a magnificent royal gown, a shining crown, and her favorite roller skates, Astrid rolled through the gates of Wagglesworth Estate at full speed.

“Make way!” shouted Buttons, Head of Royal Security.

“For danger?” asked Countess Tilly.

“For fashion moving very fast.”

Astrid swept through the grand hall, circled the fountain twice, and glided into the drawing room just as the Baroness lifted her teacup.

“Your Grace,” Astrid announced, “I believe the official story has misplaced one Lady-in-Waiting.”

The Baroness looked at the skates. She looked at the polished floor. She looked at Tilly, who had already reached for a rulebook.

“An unforgivable oversight,” the Baroness said. “Consider the record corrected.”

Astrid twirled so happily that her royal skirts floated around her like a flower.

Petunia clapped. Special Sparkle threw glitter. Buttons blew his whistle.

Tilly closed her eyes.

From that day forward, every official Wagglesworth roll call included Lady Astrid.

Usually before the sentence: “No roller skates on the grand staircase.”
