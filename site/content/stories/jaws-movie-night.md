Movie night began with one simple rule.

“Everyone has to be quiet so I can hear the movie,” Dad said.

Because Dad is blind, he follows films by listening closely to the dialogue, music, sound effects, and audio description. The household knew this.

The household also included Baroness Wagglesworth, Buttons the Bear, and Tiny Petunia.

So the rule was in immediate danger.

The Baroness selected *Jaws* because she had been assured it was about a large creature receiving the attention it deserved.

Buttons claimed the best corner of the couch.

“Da,” he said in his deepest Russian growl. “Shark is strong. But bear is stronger.”

Petunia sat in a popcorn bowl wearing a paper captain’s hat.

Dad started the movie.

The first ominous music began.

Da-dum.

The Baroness’s ears lifted.

Da-dum.

Buttons leaned forward.

Da-dum, da-dum, da-dum.

Petunia threw one kernel of popcorn into the air and shouted, “Shark!”

Dad paused the movie.

“I know,” he said. “The music is being subtle.”

They promised to behave.

The movie resumed.

For several minutes, the room remained quiet. Then Buttons began explaining his personal plan for defeating the shark.

“First, bear secures couch. Couch becomes command ship. Then—”

Dad turned his head and gave them the look.

It was a particularly impressive look because he did not even need to see them to know exactly who was talking.

Buttons lowered his voice.

“Then bear says nothing.”

The Baroness watched the dark ocean fill the screen.

“I do not care for water that contains surprises,” she whispered.

Petunia climbed out of the popcorn bowl and moved closer to Dad.

When the shark finally appeared, every member of the royal household jumped.

Popcorn flew across the room.

Buttons shouted something in Russian.

The Baroness leaped onto the back of the couch.

Petunia landed upside down in a cushion.

Dad paused the movie again.

“For people who insisted they were not scared,” he said, “you sound like the shark is in the living room.”

“We are not scared,” said the Baroness, still standing on the couch. “We are tactically elevated.”

The movie resumed one last time.

This time, they stayed quiet enough for Dad to hear every important line. Buttons guarded the popcorn. Petunia held Dad’s finger during the suspenseful parts. The Baroness remained tactically elevated until the credits rolled.

Then Dad asked, “So, did everybody enjoy it?”

“Excellent film,” Buttons declared. “Bear would win.”

Petunia removed her captain’s hat and announced that she would never go swimming again.

The Baroness climbed down from the couch with great dignity.

“The shark was moderately impressive,” she said. “But it lacked royal bearing, proper manners, and a title.”

Dad smiled.

“Baroness Jawsworth?”

Noodles considered it.

“Absolutely not.”

Movie night ended with popcorn everywhere, Petunia asleep in the bowl, Buttons still planning his couch-based naval strategy, and the Baroness refusing to walk past the bathtub without an escort.
